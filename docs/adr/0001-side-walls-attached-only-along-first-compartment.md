# Side Walls are attached only along the first Compartment

The Insert must be one Sheet with no glue, and the Dividers are Pleats in the floor. Each Divider pulls 2 × Insert Height + 10mm of floor together, so a Side Wall attached along the full Length would have to pleat along with it and would crumple. We therefore attach each Side Wall to the floor by a fold only beside Compartment 1, and Cut it free beside the Dividers and every later Compartment, where it stands on its own between the End Walls and the box.

## Considered Options

- **Side Walls attached along the full Length**: blocks the Divider Pleats.
- **Separate Divider pieces, slotted or glued in**: breaks the one-Sheet, no-glue rule.
- **Dividers across the Width instead**: only moves the problem to the End Walls.

## Consequences

- The floor beside the later Compartments is longer than the Side Wall when flat. The leftover paper is cut away as waste, so the Sheet is larger than the material that ends up in the Insert.
- Do not "fix" the Cut between a Side Wall and the floor beyond Compartment 1. It is what lets the Insert fold.
- With a single Compartment there are no Dividers, so the Side Walls are attached along its full length and there is no Waste beside them. This is the same rule, not an exception.
