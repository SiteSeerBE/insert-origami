Insert Origami

Turn one Sheet of paper or card into a board game Insert with as many Compartments as you need, with no glue.

Not the box height: subtract room for the board, rulebook and lid that go on top.

Typical: 80 gsm paper ≈ 0.1 mm, 300 gsm card ≈ 0.35 mm, grey board ≈ 1 mm or more.

Compartments mm, clear inside length

Compartment 1 is the only one the Side Walls are fixed to: make it your longest for a sturdier Insert.

Split evenly until you change a length.

    Compartment 1 isn't your longest. That works, but the Insert is sturdier if it is.
